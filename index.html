THE JUKEBOX PROJECT

A new perspective on our planet.

Jukebox is a real-time satellite sonification engine that converts NASA Earth observation imagery into harmonic, spatial music. Built for blind and low-vision explorers, perceptive researchers, and curious minds to hear how Earth’s land, air, and oceans connect.
